\noindent\textbf{Setup.} A held-out test split is embedded and clustered by two out-of-sample extensions
of the same fixed operator $\W$ (mean$\pm$std over 5 seeds; OutdoorScene 4, one seed dropped to a
degenerate view). Nystr\"om = the SVD's own training-free extension: form the new point's operator
row $\mathbf w_*$ against the training set and project it onto the training singular vectors,
$\psi(x_*)=\mathbf w_*^\top\mathbf V$. deep ($\sqrt{\bp}$-Gram) = the DDM encoder $f_\theta$ (one
MLP branch per view), the same encoder for every dataset, with no feature/affinity variants. It is
trained by $\sqrt{\bp}$-Gram matching: let $\bp$ be the stationary distribution of $\W$
($\bp^\top\W=\bp^\top$), $\A=\Pii^{1/2}\W\Pii^{-1/2}$ its $\sqrt{\bp}$-symmetrised form, and
$\mathbf G=\A\A^\top-\sqrt{\bp}\sqrt{\bp}^\top$ the target Gram (with the trivial top mode $\sqrt{\bp}$
removed). The loss makes the encoder's $\sqrt{\bp}$-scaled Gram match it,
$\min_\theta\big\lVert(\sqrt{\bp}\!\odot\!F)(\sqrt{\bp}\!\odot\!F)^\top-\mathbf G\big\rVert_F^2$,
$F=f_\theta(X)$. By Eckart--Young its optimum is the top-$k$ eigenspace of $\mathbf G$ (the diffusion-map
embedding), so the net learns that embedding as a function and extends to new points by a forward
pass. The $\sqrt{\bp}$ scaling is essential, not cosmetic: a constant (collapsed) embedding lands exactly
on the removed $\sqrt{\bp}\sqrt{\bp}^\top$ mode, so it is penalised; drop it and the encoder collapses to
a constant (verified here: AMI $\to0.02$).

\noindent\textbf{Result.} Over 5 seeds Nystr\"om and the encoder tie within one std on every
dataset (row gaps $\le0.04$, inside the $\pm0.02$--$0.10$ spreads): out-of-sample is a wash. The
single-run gaps that once looked decisive are seed noise. A faithfully-trained encoder does not beat the
SVD's free Nystr\"om extension; at best it reproduces it.
