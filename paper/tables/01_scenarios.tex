\noindent\textbf{Setup.} The map for the whole paper: each experiment is one edit to the DDM/MDT
pipeline. $\W$ is the MDT operator, $S=\mathrm{diag}(\sqrt{\bp})$ its $\sqrt{\bp}$ weighting, $\mathbf G$
the DDM Gram target, $\mathbf w_*$ the operator row of a new point $x_*$ against the training set.
Scenario 1 (Accuracy): freeze $\W$, replace its SVD readout with a net $f_\theta$ trained by
$\sqrt{\bp}$-Gram matching. Scenario 2 (Cost): freeze $\W$, drop the net, take a matrix-free
truncated \texttt{svds}. Scenario 3 (OOS): freeze $\W$, learn a map for new points and
compare it to Nystr\"om. Scenario 4 (End-to-end): make $\W(\theta)$ itself trainable under a
contrastive loss.

\noindent\textbf{Result.} Scenarios 1--3 keep $\W$ fixed and only change how its SVD embedding is read
out or extended, and all succeed or tie the classical baseline. Scenario 4 is the only one that removes the
fixed SVD target, and it collapses. Every later table instantiates one of these four rows.
